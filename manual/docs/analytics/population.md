# Population, health and safety

The demographic and social tabs of the analytics drawer. Each opens from its tile on the strip, or from **View ›
Analytics** in the desktop app.

## Population

**Population pyramid**: residents by sex in five-year bands from 0-4 to 80+, for the province or the scope you
picked. **Population, households**: residents, children, over-65s and households, month by month (for a city or a
settlement: its population, households and poor households). **Cities: population** and **Cities: household
income** compare Emmaus, Newhaven and Ithemba, or the settlements of the city you picked. **Vital events
(cumulative)**: births, deaths, marriages, emigrants and immigrants.

**Year by year** is the province's annual table, one row for each year since the start: population, births,
deaths, the crude birth and death rates per 1,000 (on the year's mean population), realised TFR, e₀ for men and
women adjusted for experience, marriages and A/E.

!!! note "Cumulative columns"
    In *Year by year*, the TFR, e₀ and A/E columns are **cumulative since the start**, not that year's own figures;
    the CBR and CDR are the year's.

## Fertility

**Age-specific fertility: basis vs realised**: births per 1,000 women by five-year band from 15-19 to 45-49, the
basis schedule against the province's own, with the basis TFR, the realised TFR and the births A/E. With a few
dozen women in each band the realised rates are noisy: the **births A/E** is the sturdier statistic.

**Births actual vs expected**: births against the births expected on the basis, by band, with women's exposure in
woman-years. **Births by year.**

The realised TFR is five times the sum, over the bands, of births per woman-year since the start.

## Health

**Illness onsets by calendar month**: the seasonality of illness, January to December (winter's respiratory
infections, summer's gastroenteritis). **Episodes by kind**. **Ill and hospitalised (monthly)**: residents ill, in
the ward, and the population's mean vitality. **Chronic conditions now (prevalence)**, for the province or the
scope, with the clinics' consultations, admissions and recoveries so far.

## Social and church

**The city councils**: every seat, by city and portfolio, its holder and settlement. **Sunday attendance**: the
share of the church-going cities' residents at the service each week (Emmaus and Ithemba; Newhaven has no
congregation). **Disputes, mediations, fights**, month by month. **Relationship ties** and **Marital status (18+)**,
for the province or the scope.

## Safety and the court

**Incidents by kind**. **Policing and court**: incidents, arrests, court cases, convictions and road accidents, the
kilometres driven and ambulance call-outs, and how the province got about (Hyperline, bus, minibus taxi and
e-hailing trips). **Recent incidents**: the last forty, with where, who handled them, the outcome and the loss;
click one to select it on the map. **Court roll**: the last forty cases, with the charge, the hearing, the verdict
and the sentence.

## Education

**Adult educational attainment** (18 and over, from none to postgraduate), for the province or the scope, and
**School vs homeschool**, month by month.

## Weather

**Weather, wind and rain**: each day's sky, its low and high, the wind (its speed and the direction it blows) and
the rain. The slider moves through the year; the province keeps the last 400 days of weather, forced days
included.

## The burial society

**View › Analytics › The burial society** (and the finance workspace's *Burial society* section): the **surplus
process** of the community's funeral and life scheme, its reserve month by month against zero, marked *solvent* or
*RUINED* with the month it happened, with premiums in and claims out; and **premiums against claims** by month.
The [actuarial workbench](actuarial.md) prices the society and puts its risk in numbers.
