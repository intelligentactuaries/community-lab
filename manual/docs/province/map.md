# The map

The map is the whole province, drawn from the simulation as it runs: every resident where they are this minute,
every car where its journey has got to, every room furnished as the province lays it out.

## Moving about

| | |
|---|---|
| **Wheel** | Zoom about the cursor. |
| **Drag** (any button) | Move the map. |
| <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd>, arrows | Move the map. |
| <kbd>=</kbd> / <kbd>−</kbd> (or the numpad's) | Zoom in and out. |
| **+**, **−**, **⌂** (right) | Zoom in, zoom out, fit the whole province. |
| **Click** | Select a person, a house (its household) or a building; on empty ground, clear the selection. |
| **Double-click** a house or building | Drop into it: the map fits it and selects it. |
| **Double-click** a person | Follow them. |

**Fly-to buttons** on the right go straight to Emmaus, Newhaven, Ithemba, Unity Provincial Airport, the Unity CBD,
Unity Park and the hospital campus; hover over one for what it is.

The **breadcrumb** at the top left (Province › City › Settlement › House › Person) shows where you are and steps
back out: click any step. <kbd>Esc</kbd> steps out one level too, after closing whatever is open on top (the scene
controls, Settings or Help, the analytics drawer).

Zoom in past the room plans and the map becomes the [3D close-up](3d.md); the **3D** button in the zoom column keeps
the map flat at every zoom instead.

## What appears as you zoom in

| Zoom | You see |
|---|---|
| The whole province | The three cities and their outlines, the roads, the Hyperline's guideway, the airport, every plot tinted in its household's colour, everyone as a dot of their shape. |
| A city | Settlement names, road names, building names. |
| A settlement | Plot numbers, then household names; door marks; the windows of buses and trains; dashed lines between people talking. |
| A house | The rooms inside every building, with their furniture; residents' household badges, mood and pregnancy marks, schooling ticks and speech bubbles. |
| A person | Room names, first names, profession codes, and the selected person's full label. |

## Reading the residents

Every resident is a **shape**, with marks that say who they are. Press <kbd>L</kbd> (or **legend** at the bottom of
the map) for the legend.

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | Personality, by the dominant Big Five trait: **circle** agreeableness (the Harmoniser), **square** conscientiousness (the Organiser), **triangle** extraversion (the Driver), **hexagon** openness (the Thinker), **diamond** neuroticism (the Sentinel), **pentagon** no trait dominant (Balanced). |
| **Colour** | Sex: blue for men and boys, orange for women and girls. |
| **Size** | Age: babies smallest, then young children, children, teenagers and adults. |
| **Ticks** on the top edge | Schooling, from none to five (none, primary, secondary, matric, tertiary, postgraduate). |
| **Ring** | Health: red when ill or injured, a thicker red when critical, amber while recovering; a dashed grey ring when grieving. |
| **Dot**, top right | Mood: green, grey, amber or red. |
| **Badge**, bottom right | The household's colour. |
| **Inner dot** | A pregnancy of more than three months. |
| **!** | Involved in an incident or a dispute, or has spotted an intruder. |
| **Letters** inside the shape | Profession, close up: Cpt pilot, Bus bus driver, Hmb e-hailing driver, Pas pastor, Dr doctor, RN nurse, Tch teacher, SAPS police, Mag magistrate, Clk clerk, Shp shopkeeper, Vnd vendor, Frm farmer, Fld farm worker, Bld builder, Off office worker, Bnk bank officer, Adv attorney, Acc accountant, DMO district medical officer, CB commercial bank officer, Txi taxi driver, Dom domestic worker, Hme homemaker, Ret retired, Stu student. |

People inside a vehicle are drawn as the vehicle, and people away from the province are not drawn.

## Households and places

Each of the province's plots has a **fixed colour**, worn by its fence and tint, by the badge on every one of its
residents, and by its chips in the lists and the inspector. Select a household (or any of its members) and every
member gets a ring in that colour wherever they are in the province.

A place with **nobody in it is drawn slightly greyed**: a house whose household is out at work, school or church, a
shop after hours, a church on a weekday, a parked car, an empty bus. The colour comes back when someone is inside.

## Vehicles and other marks

| Mark | What it is |
|---|---|
| Car, bakkie | Households' cars and pickups, parked at home or in bays beside the buildings they serve. |
| Yellow minibus | A minibus taxi. |
| Blue bus | A bus on one of the four lines into the terminus. |
| Teal car with a roof sign | A Hamba e-hailing car: the sign is green while the driver is online, amber on a surge-priced trip, grey offline. |
| Dark blue car | A police patrol car; its light flashes while it is moving. |
| White car with a red cross | An ambulance. |
| Pale capsule with a blue nose | The Hyperline. At speed it leaves a streak along the guideway: a hop between stations takes about a second of simulated time, so the train is drawn gliding between platforms. |
| Aeroplane | On the apron or the runway only: aircraft in flight are off the map. |
| Dashed line between two people | A conversation; red when it is tense. |
| Speech bubble | A conversation's latest line. A red edge is a tense exchange, a green edge singing. |
| ♪ | A voice in a hymn. |
| Pulsing ring | An incident: red, or blue for a stranger asking for help. |
| Orange flicker, green line, brown shape, dark square | A veld fire, a snake, a stray dog, an intruder. |

## Following someone

**Double-click** a person, or select them and press <kbd>F</kbd> (or the target button on the right, or **follow** in
the inspector). The camera stays with them as they go about their day. Dragging, zooming, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or
<kbd>F</kbd> again ends the follow.
